Experimental audio lab. Emulators, firmware mods and audio tooling.

- **[stemd](https://github.com/NTSR-Division/stemd)** · Split any track into stems, on your own machine.
- **[cdj3k-emu](https://github.com/NTSR-Division/cdj3k-emu)** · A CDJ's own firmware, booted inside QEMU.
- **[toska-loader](https://toska-loader.com)** · A mod loader for the CDJ-3000.

NTSR (stands for: noise-to-signal ratio). More at [ntsr.dev](https://ntsr.dev), or write to [contact@ntsr.dev](mailto:contact@ntsr.dev).
